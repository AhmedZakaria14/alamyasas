# Movion reproduction design brief

## Ground-truth reference
هذا المشروع استنساخ مباشر لموقع Movion Template Kit المرجعي. المرجع الحاكم هو موقع `templates.sparklethings.com/movion/template-kit/` من حيث ترتيب الأقسام، نظام التنقل، النصوص الأساسية، ألوان الواجهة، صور الحركة، أزرار الدعوة إلى الإجراء، وبنية التذييل. لا تُطبّق إعادة تفسير إبداعية تُضعف التطابق البصري.

## Design Movement
Contemporary editorial service branding: a restrained mix of Swiss editorial typography, utility signage, and premium moving-company photography.

## Core Principles
1. Use large editorial headlines and generous whitespace to create confidence.
2. Keep the palette disciplined: warm white, charcoal, and a vivid transport red.
3. Let photography carry the emotional story while overlays preserve legibility.
4. Use compact pill CTAs and small uppercase labels as wayfinding signals.

## Color Philosophy
Red is the motion signal: it marks action, reliability, and the human energy of moving. Charcoal supplies trust and contrast; warm white keeps the service approachable rather than industrial. The signature red is `#ee1d38`.

## Layout Paradigm
Use wide, edge-to-edge photography for hero and banner moments, then switch to asymmetric editorial sections with image-and-copy pairings. Avoid a repetitive centered-card grid; content should breathe across the page and align to a strong horizontal rhythm.

## Signature Elements
The site repeats red arrow-like CTA pills, uppercase red eyebrow labels, and thin charcoal dividers. Statistics appear as oversized numerals with compact labels, echoing transport wayfinding.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel direct and assured: buttons compress slightly on press, links reveal a red underline, and navigation remains obvious on every page. Mobile navigation should open as a simple, high-contrast drawer.

## Animation
Use short 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, links, drawer, and cards. Hero content enters with a subtle upward reveal; no decorative motion should compete with the photography. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System
Use `Manrope` for body and UI text, with `DM Sans` for display headlines to echo the reference’s geometric, friendly proportions. Headlines are large and light-to-medium weight; eyebrows and navigation use uppercase or tight tracking.

## Brand Essence
A dependable moving partner for households and businesses that want every move handled with calm precision. Personality: reliable, energetic, reassuring.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and confident. CTAs are active and specific; microcopy is warm and practical. Example lines: `Move with confidence.` and `Tell us where you’re going.` Avoid generic filler.

## Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated abstract red box-and-arrow mark beside the `Movion` wordmark, with a simple geometric word treatment rather than a default logo image. The mark also serves as the favicon-scale brand symbol.

## Signature Brand Color
`#ee1d38` — Movion Red.
